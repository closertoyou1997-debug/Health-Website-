First Aid & Rapid Emergency Protocols

Urgent Action

Clear, step-by-step instructions for acute situations and minor injuries. Understand when to contact emergency services immediately versus when basic bystander first aid is appropriate.

Rapid Triage Rule: When to Call 911 Immediately

Always call emergency services first before initiating treatment if you observe any of the following critical red flags:

1. Severe Breathing Distress Gasping, unable to speak, blue lips or fingertips.
2. Suspected Acute Stroke Sudden facial droop, arm weakness, slurred speech (FAST).
3. Uncontrollable Bleeding Spurting or continuous bleeding soaking through bandages.
4. Unconsciousness / Arrest Unresponsive and not breathing normally. Start CPR now.

About MediCare AI — Purpose, Architecture & Ethics

MediCare AI is a technology demonstration created to showcase how modern web engineering, responsible generative AI guardrails, and responsive client-side architectures can democratize health literacy and daily wellness habits.

Project Purpose & Objectives

The objective of MediCare AI is to address three prominent challenges in digital health:

  • Complex Medical Terminology: Translating clinical jargon into layman-accessible language.
  • Behavioral Habit Consistency: Friction-free daily logging for hydration, sleep, exercise, and mood.
  • Emergency Triage Awareness: Ensuring users recognize acute red flags and contact emergency services.

Technical Architecture & Stack
